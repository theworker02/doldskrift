# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Doldskrift a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project and research questions
- Keeping security claims honest (Open / Neural are not encryption)

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Presenting Doldskrift as encryption or encouraging harmful misuse of the Protected track before AEAD ships
- Sustained disruption of discussions or bad-faith “gotchas” that ignore documented honesty bounds

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Maintainers who do not enforce in good faith may be removed from the project team.

## Scope

This Code applies within project spaces (GitHub issues, discussions, PRs, and official pages) and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers via the security / private channel described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), or by opening a confidential maintainer contact if one is listed on the project site. For vulnerability reports, use GitHub Security Advisories when available.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly. Consequences may include warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban from project spaces, depending on severity and pattern of behavior.
