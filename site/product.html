Where it helps

Grounded use cases — not invented customers.

  • Machine-readable displays and agent debugging surfaces
  • Camera-readable device coordination experiments
  • Visual telemetry and HCI research
  • Air-gap demonstrations of optical machine channels
  • Printed machine-oriented media

Where it is not

Not encryption. Not a substitute for QR when you need a ubiquitous scanner ecosystem. Not an accessible human UI — provide a semantic channel whenever people must understand the content (safety, legal, medical, consent).
